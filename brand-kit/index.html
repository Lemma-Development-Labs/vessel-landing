01

The mark

A hull seen head-on, cut by its waterline. The circle is the vault; the line is the hedge — flat, level, and the one thing you're asked to verify. It reads as a plimsoll line, a horizon, and a zero. That's the whole idea: net delta ≈ 0.00.

STROKE WEIGHT IS PROPORTIONAL — 2.5/32 OF THE MARK'S HEIGHT. NEVER SET IT TO A FIXED PX VALUE ACROSS SIZES.

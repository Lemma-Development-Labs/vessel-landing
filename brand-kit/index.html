01

The mark

The circle is the vessel. The horizontal rule is the waterline — the line between Hull above and Ballast below. The plumb dropping from centre is the hedge: the leg that holds the whole thing upright, and the only stroke that carries colour.

CONSTRUCTION GRID
40 × 40
HULL CIRCLE
r 14.4 · stroke 2.6
WATERLINE
x 3 → 37 at y 20
PLUMB
y 20 → 34.4 at x 20 · signal
CAPS
butt · one weight throughout

STROKE WEIGHT IS PROPORTIONAL — 2.6/40 OF THE MARK'S HEIGHT. NEVER SET IT TO A FIXED PX VALUE ACROSS SIZES. THE WATERLINE OVERSHOOTS THE CIRCLE ON BOTH SIDES; THAT OVERSHOOT IS THE MARK.

Clearspace & minimum size

VESSEL
x x

Clearspace x = half the mark's height on every side. Nothing enters it — no type, no rules, no other logos, no image edges.

MARK MIN
18 PX · 5 MM
VESSEL

BELOW HORIZONTAL MIN, DROP THE WORDMARK AND USE THE MARK ALONE. BELOW 18 PX THE PLUMB CLOSES UP — USE THE FAVICON BUILD INSTEAD.

05

Don't

The mark's whole job is to look like a level line with a plumb hanging true. Everything below tilts it, crowds it, or dresses it up.

✕ RECOLOUR THE HULL
✕ ROTATE OR TILT
✕ STRETCH OR SQUASH
✕ SHORTEN THE PLUMB
✕ GLOWS OR SHADOWS
VESSEL ✕ RE-SET IN ANOTHER FACE
